Overview
THE ATMOSFERA is a no-code Earth observation project that detects atmospheric and climate trends over Kuala Lumpur using NASA's open Earth data, with technology adaptable for future Moon and Mars habitats.

Our mission: From Kedah's paddy fields to the Moon - protecting Earth's atmosphere today, preparing for the next frontier tomorrow.

NASA Worldview (worldview.earthdata.nasa.gov) - Real-time satellite imagery

Problem
Kuala Lumpur faces increasing flood and urban heat risks. We need simple, no-code tools to detect atmospheric trends early, using free NASA data - technology that can also protect future Moon habitats.

Solution (No-Code)
Use NASA Worldview to monitor KL atmospheric trends (cloud, rain rate)
Visualize data in dashboard (Gold prototype)
Storytelling: Link KL's flood protection to Moon habitat atmospheric shielding
No coding required - screenshot, visualization, storytelling
Impact
Protects 2M people in KL from flood/heat early warning
Educational tool for Kedah students to learn NASA data
Scalable: Can be used for any city + Moon/Mars habitat atmospheric monitoring
Aligns with NASA's Earth Science Division mission
How It Helps Moon/Mars (The Next Frontier)
Technology to monitor and protect atmosphere on Earth (KL) = same technology needed to monitor and protect habitats on Moon/Mars. Water recycling, heat shield, flood barrier concepts transfer directly.
